## WEEKLY OVERVIEW & NARRATIVE

The first full trading week of 2026 was broadly positive, with all three major indices posting gains and the S&P 500 and Dow setting fresh all-time highs. The week was dominated by several major themes:

1. **CES 2026 and AI Mania** — Nvidia's Jensen Huang keynote on Jan 5-6 unveiled the Vera Rubin platform. Memory chip stocks (Micron, SanDisk) exploded higher on AI data center demand.
2. **U.S. Military Action in Venezuela** — On Saturday Jan 3, U.S. forces captured Venezuelan President Maduro. Markets shrugged it off on Monday; oil prices actually fell on oversupply.

4. **"Great Rotation" Beginning** — Small caps (Russell 2000) and cyclicals outperformed mega-cap tech. The Magnificent Seven underperformed the broader market.
5. **Jobs Report (Jan 9)** — December nonfarm payrolls came in weak at +50K (vs +73K expected), but unemployment fell to 4.4%. Fed rate cut odds for January dropped to ~3%.

## SANTA CLAUS RALLY / JANUARY EFFECT ANALYSIS

### Santa Claus Rally (Last 5 days of Dec + First 2 of Jan)
- The S&P 500 finished lower on the last day of 2025 (Dec 31) but edged higher on Jan 2. The Santa Claus Rally period technically ended Jan 2. Results were mixed — the phenomenon had failed in 2023 and 2024.

### January Effect / First Five Days Indicator
- The S&P 500 gained more than 1% in the first five trading days (Jan 2-9). Historically, when the S&P 500 is positive in the first 5 trading days, it has risen for the full year about 83% of the time with an average annual gain of ~14%.
- The "January Barometer" (full month performance) ended positive: S&P 500 gained 1.44% in January 2026.
- Small caps dramatically outperformed, consistent with the "January Effect" where smaller, beaten-down stocks rally after year-end tax-loss selling.

### Was There a Santa Rally Continuation?
**YES** — The first week of 2026 was strongly positive, continuing the late-2025 seasonal bullishness. However, the "Great Rotation" was the dominant feature: small caps and cyclicals led while Magnificent Seven stocks lagged. The Russell 2000 surged 3.1% in just the first 3 trading days, far outpacing the S&P 500 (+0.99%) and Nasdaq (+0.26%) in that same window.

### Key Driver: Rate Cuts Helping Small Caps
- Three consecutive Fed rate cuts in Q4 2025 brought the federal funds rate to 3.50%-3.75%.
- ~50% of small-cap debt is floating-rate, so lower rates directly boost their bottom lines.
- This supported the rotation into value/small-cap from growth/mega-cap.
